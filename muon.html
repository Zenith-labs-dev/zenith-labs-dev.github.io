Muon

a music player for the library you already own.

Muon is a free desktop music player, forked from the excellent Tauon. No account, no ads, no subscription.

Why I built this

I wanted a music player that treats a local library as a first-class thing: fast to open, good with big collections, and not trying to sell me anything. Tauon was already most of the way there, so Muon starts from it and goes in the direction I wanted to take it.

Muon is free and stays free. It is built on Tauon's work and released under the same open-source licence, so the source is there for anyone to read, fork, or improve. If you find it useful, a donation helps keep it moving.

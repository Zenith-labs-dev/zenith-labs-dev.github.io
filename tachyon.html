Why I built this

I read on the sofa and listen on the move, and I was tired of hunting for my place every time I switched between the two. I wanted one app that knows where I am in a book, whichever way I'm getting through it.

Tachyon is that app. You pay once and it's yours: no subscription and no account needed just to open a book you already own.
